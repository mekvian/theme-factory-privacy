Theme Factory Privacy Policy

Developer: MEKVIAN. Effective date: 12 October 2026.
Contact: mekvian@proton.me.

Scope

This policy describes the Theme Factory Android application's local design studio. The app lets you preview five design collections, customize a local icon concept, review it and export design files. Exporting a design does not install a system theme.

Local information

Your selected collection, studio tab and customized icon settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Exported design archives are created in the app's storage and are shared or saved only when you choose an export action. The local studio does not require an account and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Sharing and optional websites

The local studio does not send your design settings to a developer server. If you choose Android's sharing or saving controls, your selected destination receives the exported file; its privacy practices apply. The default production build has no configured web studio endpoint and does not request Internet access. A future build that connects an online studio must identify that service and update its privacy and store disclosures before release. This policy does not describe the separate developer-operated web application.

Device access

The app does not request contacts, location, microphone or camera access. It does not require access to unrelated files on your device. Android's file and sharing controls grant access only to the export you select.

Wallpaper editor

When you choose a wallpaper, Theme Factory shows the approved image and asks you to confirm the selected screen. After confirmation, the app uses Android’s normal Set wallpaper permission to send only that image to the system wallpaper service with the specific home or lock target. Android fits the image to the screen; there is no separate system crop editor. Canceling does not apply a wallpaper. The app does not read your existing wallpaper or personal photos.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Files you saved outside the app must be deleted from the destination you selected. Android device backup may preserve app settings according to your device's backup settings and provider policy. The developer does not operate an account or a cloud design backup for the local studio.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you send so that we can answer your request. Do not include passwords, private documents or other sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact address above.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date when the app's data practices change.
